HA LONG BAY CRUISING AREA
Location: Quang Ninh Province, Vietnam | Coords: 20.900N, 107.183E
UNESCO World Heritage since 1994 | Area: 1,553 km2 | Islands: 1,969
Floating villages, Hang Sung Sot caves, Ti Top Island, kayaking grottoes.
Best season: Oct-Apr. Avoid July-Sept (typhoon season).
