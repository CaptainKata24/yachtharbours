ROCK ISLANDS SOUTHERN LAGOON
Location: Between Koror and Peleliu, Republic of Palau
Coordinates: 7.150N, 134.380E
UNESCO: World Heritage Site (2012) — combined natural and cultural criteria
Type: Protected marine park — anchorages within permit area
Permit: Koror State Rock Island permit required — $50/10 days
HIGHLIGHTS: 445 uninhabited limestone mushroom-shaped islands covered in jungle.
The most unique landscape in Micronesia — possibly on Earth.
Milky Way lagoon — white limestone powder bottom creates natural spa.
German Channel — famous Manta Ray cleaning station.
Big Drop-Off — legendary wall dive to 300m+. Pellucid Lagoon.
Blue Corner — arguably the world's best drift dive. Blue Holes.
Helmet, Zero and other WWII wrecks accessible from Rock Islands.
70 islands and specific reserves are completely off limits — check with harbourmaster.
