HELEN REEF NATIONAL PARK
Location: 340nm southwest of Koror, Republic of Palau
Coordinates: 2.895N, 131.779E (2deg53.097N, 131deg46.715E)
Type: Remote atoll — National Park | NOT a port of entry
Lagoon: 10-mile long, internal remains of submerged volcano rim
Island: One small island (3m above sea level) covered in trees — NW tip of lagoon
Rangers: Small National Park Ranger station on island | Monitor VHF Ch16
Anchorage: 15m on broken coral and sand — rangers will assist
Entry: Single safe passage mid-way down western edge of reef
HIGHLIGHTS: Extraordinary bird and turtle sanctuary. Loggerhead and Green sea turtles.
Legendary fishing, snorkelling and diving in pristine atoll lagoon.
Rangers welcome yacht company — extremely remote, rarely visited.
NOTE: NOT a port of entry — cannot clear into/out of Palau here.
~340nm from Koror on route to/from Indonesia.
