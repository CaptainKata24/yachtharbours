JELLYFISH LAKE (Ongeim'l Tketau)
Location: Eil Malk Island, Rock Islands, Koror State, Palau
Coordinates: 7.163N, 134.376E
Type: Marine lake — world-famous snorkelling experience
Access: Rock Island permit ($50/10 days) + Jellyfish Lake surcharge ($50) = $100 total
Or: Via Sam's Tours (RBYC) — recommended package includes transport and guide
HIGHLIGHTS: Marine lake containing millions of non-stinging golden jellyfish (Mastigias papua).
Jellyfish evolved without stingers as no predators in the lake.
Twice-daily migration following the sun across the lake. Unforgettable experience.
Scuba diving prohibited (depth below thermocline is anoxic and toxic to divers).
Snorkelling only. One of the world's most unique wildlife encounters.
Note: Lake closed periodically for jellyfish population recovery.
