MALAKAL HARBOUR
Location: Malakal Island, Koror, Republic of Palau
Coordinates: 7.318N, 134.453E | VHF: Ch16
Type: Main port of entry — commercial and yacht harbour
Approaches: 3 approaches — 2 from east, 1 from west (ships/deep draft use western)
Recommended approach for yachts: Southern Malakal channel (easier than Ngel channel)
Depth: Adequate for most yacht drafts in main channel
CIQ: YES — all vessels must clear here first (no stopping at other islands before check-in)
HIGHLIGHTS: Koror town — restaurants, supermarkets, dive shops, chandlery.
Palau International Airport 15 min from Koror. Regular flights to Manila, Guam, Tokyo.
Base for exploring Rock Islands by day.
Compact, friendly capital city with excellent provisioning.
