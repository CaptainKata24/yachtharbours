SIHANOUKVILLE PORT (Kampong Saom)
Location: Sihanoukville, Preah Sihanouk Province, Cambodia
Coordinates: 10.628N, 103.521E
Type: Main commercial port + yacht check-in point
CIQ: YES — primary yacht entry/exit for Cambodia
Check-in: KAMSAB building (Kampuchea Shipping & Brokerage), behind concrete pier
Note: Do NOT enter port — proceed 1 mile south to Victory Beach first
Visa: Pre-obtained e-visa highly recommended (www.evisa.gov.kh)
Visa on arrival: Possible but can cost up to $150/person processing fee
Stay: 30-day visa on arrival | Extensions: Available
No current restrictions on destinations or itinerary within Cambodia
HIGHLIGHTS: Kampong Saom Bay — protected anchorage. Gateway to Cambodian islands.
Sihanoukville — major Chinese investment, casino development, beach resorts.
32 offshore islands accessible from here.
