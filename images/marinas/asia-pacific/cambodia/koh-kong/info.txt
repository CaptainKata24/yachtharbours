KOH KONG
Location: Koh Kong Province, Southwest Cambodia (Thai border area)
Coordinates: 11.615N, 102.984E
Type: Border town + anchorage — alternative entry point from Thailand
Check-in: Cambodian Tourist Office assists with transport to border post
Entry: Simple — suitable for yachts arriving from Thailand
Koh Kong town and island joined by bridge
HIGHLIGHTS: Cardamom Mountains wilderness. Tatai River mangroves and waterfalls.
Very few tourists — authentic Cambodian experience. Excellent birdwatching.
Gateway to the Cambodian coast from the west.
Koh Kong island beaches — wild and remote.
