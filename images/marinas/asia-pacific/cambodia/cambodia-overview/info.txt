CAMBODIA CRUISING OVERVIEW
Coastline: Short Gulf of Siam coastline + 32 offshore islands
Best kept secret: One of SE Asia's most undervisited cruising destinations
Main Port: Sihanoukville / Kampong Saom (CIQ entry/exit)
Alternative Entry: Koh Kong (from Thailand)
Visa: E-visa recommended (www.evisa.gov.kh) | 30 days on arrival
Highlights: Sparsely populated silica sand beaches, bioluminescent bays,
pristine reefs, Koh Rong island group, Song Saa Private Island resort
Connections: Flights from Sihanoukville to Bangkok, Singapore, Hong Kong, China
Inland: Angkor Wat (UNESCO) — 4 hours drive from Sihanoukville
Best season: November-April (NE monsoon, dry season)
Note: Significant Chinese casino/resort investment ongoing in Sihanoukville
