KOH RONG GROUP
Location: 15 miles offshore from Sihanoukville, Preah Sihanouk Province, Cambodia
Coordinates: 10.720N, 103.240E
Type: Island group — anchorages and guesthouses
Islands: Koh Rong (2nd largest in Cambodia), Koh Rong Samloem + 10 smaller islands
Villages on Koh Rong: Koh Tuich, Dam Dkeuw, Prek Svay, Soksan
Guesthouses: 15+ | Restaurants: Multiple | Transport: Fast boat from Sihanoukville
HIGHLIGHTS: One of Southeast Asia's best-kept secret cruising destinations.
Pristine silica sand beaches. Bioluminescent plankton at night.
World-class snorkelling and diving on offshore reefs.
Song Saa Private Island — ultra-luxury resort (2 of Cambodia's finest islands).
Relatively undeveloped — authentic, sparsely populated.
