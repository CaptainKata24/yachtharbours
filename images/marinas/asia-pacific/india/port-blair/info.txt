PORT BLAIR — ANDAMAN ISLANDS
Location: South Andaman Island, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, India
Coordinates: 11.662N, 92.746E | VHF: Ch16
Type: Primary port of entry for Andaman Islands
CIQ: YES — mandatory check-in for all visiting yachts
Clearance: Obtainable within 24 hours (if compliant, well-presented, courteous)
Agent: Using a licensed Andaman Islands yacht agent strongly recommended
Advance booking: Arrange clearances well in advance of arrival
Shore Power: Available | Fuel: Available | Basic repairs: Available
HIGHLIGHTS: Cellular Jail (Kala Pani) — historic colonial prison, UNESCO nomination.
Corbyn's Cove beach. Anthropological Museum. Andaman Water Sports Complex.
Gateway to entire Andaman island chain.
RESTRICTIONS: Nicobars remain completely off limits. Radio check-in required daily.
Restrictions protect rare biodiversity and 6 indigenous tribes.
Sentinelese tribe — hostile to outsiders, protected by Indian government.
Best season: Mid-December to end March (NE monsoon).
DISTANCE: ~450nm northwest of Phuket, Thailand.
