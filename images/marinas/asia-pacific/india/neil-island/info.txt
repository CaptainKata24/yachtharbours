NEIL ISLAND (Shaheed Dweep)
Location: Andaman Islands, India
Coordinates: 11.834N, 93.041E
Type: Anchorage — quiet, unspoiled island
Permits: Required as part of Andaman cruising clearance from Port Blair
HIGHLIGHTS: Natural rock formations — Natural Bridge. Lakshamanpur Beach.
Very quiet and relaxed compared to Havelock. Bicycle rental available.
Excellent snorkelling and reef walking at low tide.
Small local fishing community. Basic guesthouses and restaurants.
45 min ferry from Port Blair.
