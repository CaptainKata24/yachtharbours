ANDAMAN & NICOBAR ISLANDS — CRUISING OVERVIEW
Location: Bay of Bengal, India (~450nm NW of Phuket, Thailand)
Territory: Indian Union Territory | Capital: Port Blair
Islands: 325 total (Andaman + Nicobar) | Open to yachts: Selected Andamans only
Nicobars: COMPLETELY OFF LIMITS to visiting vessels
Entry: Port Blair only | Clearance: Within 24hrs if compliant
Daily radio check-in: Mandatory throughout cruising permit
Agent: Use licensed Andaman Islands yacht agent — essential
Best season: Mid-December to end of March
Visa: Indian visa required in advance (no VOA for yacht crew in most cases)
FREE download: Andaman Islands pdf booklet — www.southeastasiapilot.com/THE-ANDAMAN-ISLANDS.pdf
HIGHLIGHTS: Pristine biodiversity. Six protected indigenous tribes.
Radhanagar Beach (Havelock) — one of Asia's finest. World-class diving.
Coral reefs, mangroves, tropical forest. Dugongs and Nicobar pigeons.
Useful as stopover on passage: Phuket → Sri Lanka → Maldives → Red Sea
