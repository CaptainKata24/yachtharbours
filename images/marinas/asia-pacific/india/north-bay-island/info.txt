NORTH BAY ISLAND (Coral Island)
Location: Near Port Blair, South Andaman, India
Coordinates: 11.703N, 92.778E
Type: Day anchorage / excursion island from Port Blair
HIGHLIGHTS: Famous for glass-bottom boat rides — outstanding coral visibility.
Sea-walking experiences. Snorkelling. Scuba diving intro dives.
Lighthouse on island. Accessible from Port Blair by 20-min ferry.
One of Andamans' most popular day trips.
