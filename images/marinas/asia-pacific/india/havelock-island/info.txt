HAVELOCK ISLAND (Swaraj Dweep)
Location: Andaman Islands, India
Coordinates: 11.980N, 93.025E
Type: Anchorage — most popular tourist island in Andamans
Permits: Required as part of Andaman cruising clearance from Port Blair
HIGHLIGHTS: Radhanagar Beach — rated one of Asia's best beaches (Time Magazine).
Elephant Beach — excellent snorkelling. Glass-bottom boat tours.
Havelock town — restaurants, guesthouses, dive operators.
World-class scuba diving — Barracuda City, The Wall, Aquarium.
Lush tropical forest interior. Deer and bird watching.
Ferry connections to Port Blair.
