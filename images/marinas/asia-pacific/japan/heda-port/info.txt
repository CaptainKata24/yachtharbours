HEDA PORT
Location: Heda, Izu Peninsula, Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan
Coordinates: 34.9833°N, 138.7667°E
Region: Tokai / Izu Peninsula (Western coast)
Type: Fishing village port + anchorage
HIGHLIGHTS: One of the most remote and scenic ports on the Izu Peninsula.
Historic — Russian ship Diana was wrecked here in 1855. Japanese/Russian friendship history.
Sagami Bay views. Incredible seafood — deep-sea crab.
Spectacular mountain backdrop. Very few tourists — authentic fishing village.
