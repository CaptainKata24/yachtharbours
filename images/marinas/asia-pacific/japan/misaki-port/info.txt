MISAKI PORT
Location: Miura City, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan
Coordinates: 35.1333°N, 139.6167°E
Region: Kanto (Miura Peninsula, Tokyo Bay entrance)
Type: Fishing port + marina
HIGHLIGHTS: Japan's most famous port for fresh tuna — rivals Tsukiji.
Misaki Maguro (tuna) Festival each spring. Daily tuna auctions.
Scenic Miura Peninsula. Close to Jogashima Island lighthouse.
Day trip from Tokyo (90 min). Popular weekend destination.
