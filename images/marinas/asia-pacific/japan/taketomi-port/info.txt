TAKETOMI PORT (Yaeyama Islands)
Location: Taketomi Island, Yaeyama Islands, Okinawa Prefecture, Japan
Coordinates: 24.3233°N, 124.0733°E
Region: Ryukyu Islands / Yaeyama (Southern Okinawa)
Type: Small island ferry port + anchorage
HIGHLIGHTS: Taketomi Island — traditional Ryukyuan village preserved as cultural heritage.
Buffalo-cart tours through star-sand beach villages. Red tile roofs and stone walls.
15 min ferry from Ishigaki. UNESCO World Heritage candidate.
Gateway to Iriomote Island (jungle, mangroves, rare wildcats).
Exceptional coral reefs and diving.
