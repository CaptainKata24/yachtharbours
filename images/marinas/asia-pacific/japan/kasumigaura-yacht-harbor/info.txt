KASUMIGAURA YACHT HARBOR
Location: Ibaraki Prefecture, Japan (Lake Kasumigaura)
Coordinates: 36.0000°N, 140.3833°E
Region: Kanto (Ibaraki)
Type: Freshwater lake marina — Japan's 2nd largest lake
Berths: Visitor berths | Shore Power: Yes | Sailing School: Yes
HIGHLIGHTS: Japan's 2nd largest lake (168 km²) — superb flat-water sailing.
Annual Kasumigaura Regatta. Bird watching — migratory wildfowl.
Lotus flowers in summer. Easy day trip from Tokyo (60 min by train).
