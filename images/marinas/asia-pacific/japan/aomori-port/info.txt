AOMORI PORT
Location: Aomori City, Aomori Prefecture, Japan
Coordinates: 40.8333°N, 140.7500°E
Region: Tohoku (Northern Honshu)
Type: Commercial and cruise port + marina
Tripadvisor: Listed in Japan's top marinas
HIGHLIGHTS: Gateway to Tsugaru Strait and Hokkaido crossings.
Famous Aomori Nebuta Festival (August) — spectacular floats and drumming.
Apple orchards and fresh seafood (scallops, sea urchin).
Close to Towada-Hachimantai National Park.
