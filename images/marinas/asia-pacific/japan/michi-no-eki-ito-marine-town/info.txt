MICHI-NO-EKI ITO MARINE TOWN
Location: Ito City, Shizuoka Prefecture, Japan (Izu Peninsula)
Region: Tokai / Izu Peninsula
Type: Umi no Eki (Sea Station) + Tourist Complex
Tripadvisor Rating: 3.7 (715 reviews) — #2 on Japan Marina list
Status: Open (tourist and marina facilities)
