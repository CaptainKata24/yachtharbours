LABUAN BAJO MARINA
Location: Labuan Bajo, Flores, East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia
Coordinates: 8.496S, 119.860E
Type: Port and marina — gateway to Komodo National Park
CIQ: Yes | Fuel: By arrangement | Shore Power: Limited
HIGHLIGHTS: Gateway to Komodo National Park (UNESCO World Heritage).
Komodo Dragons — world's largest lizard, found only here.
Exceptional diving — Komodo, Rinca, Padar islands. Pink Beach.
Manta rays in Manta Alley. World-class drift diving.
Increasingly popular superyacht destination. New AYANA Resort nearby.
Best season: April-December. Komodo Park entry permit required.
