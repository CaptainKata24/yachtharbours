MERIDIAN ADVENTURE MARINA — RAJA AMPAT
Location: Waisai, Raja Ampat, West Papua, Indonesia
Coordinates: 0.440S, 130.870E
Berths: Vessels up to 25m | Hotel: Yes | Restaurant: Yes | Laundry: Yes | Pool: Yes

HIGHLIGHTS: Raja Ampat — the world's #1 marine biodiversity hotspot.
Over 600 coral species and 1,700+ fish species. Manta ray cleaning stations.
Misool, Waigeo, Salawati and Batanta islands — stunning limestone karst.
Waisai is the capital and main entry port for Raja Ampat.
Access from Sorong (gateway city) by ferry or liveaboard.
UNESCO potential — extraordinary underwater wilderness.
