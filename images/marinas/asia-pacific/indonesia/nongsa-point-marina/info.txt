NONGSA POINT MARINA
Location: Nongsa, Batam Island, Riau Islands Province, Indonesia
Coordinates: 1.185N, 104.100E | VHF: Ch69
Berths: 78 | Max LOA: 40m+ | Superyachts: Yes | Fuel: Yes | F&B: 1
Haul-out: No | Repairs: No | Chandlery: No | CIQ: YES (international entry/exit port)
Shore Power: Yes | Water: Yes (non-potable throughout Indonesia)
Website: www.nongsapointmarina.com
HIGHLIGHTS: Most established marina in Indonesia. 20 min ferry to Singapore.
Dedicated clearance port — all international entry/exit paperwork handled here.
Resort hotel and villas on-site. 9-hole golf course. Tennis courts.
NOTES: Indonesia-wide — No dedicated fuel docks (fuel by barge or jerry can at most
marinas). Water non-potable throughout archipelago. Very limited yacht infrastructure
nationwide. Batavia/Ancol and Nongsa are the only viable entry marinas.
