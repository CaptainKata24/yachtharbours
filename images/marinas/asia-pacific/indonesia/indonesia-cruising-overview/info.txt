INDONESIA CRUISING OVERVIEW
Location: Republic of Indonesia — 17,508 islands, 5,150km east-west span
Key Ports: Nongsa (Batam/entry), Jakarta, Bali/Benoa, Lombok, Komodo, Raja Ampat
Marina Infrastructure: Very limited — state-owned, historically undeveloped
Fuel: No dedicated fuel docks — fuel by barge or jerry can at most marinas
Water: Non-potable throughout the entire archipelago
Entry Ports (foreign yachts): Nongsa Point (Batam), Bali/Benoa, Kupang (Timor)
Cruising Permit: CAIT (Cruising Permit) required in advance from Jakarta
Visa: Visa on Arrival available for most nationalities at entry ports
Cruising Route (E-W): Malacca Strait → Riau Islands → Java → Bali → Lombok →
  Komodo → Flores → Timor → (exit to Darwin, Australia)
Cruising Route (N-S): Philippines → Sulawesi → Banda Sea → Raja Ampat
Best Season: April-October (SE season) for eastern routes
Typhoons: Indonesia is outside the typhoon belt
