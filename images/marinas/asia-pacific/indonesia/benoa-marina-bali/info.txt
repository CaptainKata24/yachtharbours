BALI MARINA — BENOA
Location: Benoa Harbour, South Bali, Indonesia
Coordinates: 8.748S, 115.215E | VHF: Ch77
Berths: 30 | Superyachts: Yes | Fuel: Yes | F&B: 2 | CIQ: YES
Haul-out: No | Chandlery: No | Repairs: No
3 floating docks of 80m length | Shore power: Yes | Water: Non-potable
Owned: Government (PT Pelindo III) | Contact well in advance via yacht agent
Website: www.benoamarinabali.com
HIGHLIGHTS: Only viable superyacht destination in Bali. 
Bali — the Island of the Gods. Ubud rice terraces, Tanah Lot temple, Uluwatu.
World-class surfing at Kuta, Seminyak, Uluwatu. Hindu temple culture.
Gateway to Nusa Penida marine park — Mola mola (sunfish) and Manta rays.
NOTES: Government-owned, hard to book — always use a yacht agent. Fuel by truck.
Water non-potable. Frequently full — advance booking absolutely essential.
