MYANMAR (BURMA) CRUISING OVERVIEW
Coastline: Tanintharyi coast — 800+ island Mergui Archipelago
Main Entry: Kawthong (from Ranong, Thailand)
Formal Marinas: None — all anchorages
Permit: Required — use licensed Myanmar yacht agent
Best season: November-April | Avoid: May-October (SW monsoon)
Political Status (2026): Under military junta since Feb 2021.
Check latest FCO (UK) / State Department (US) travel advisories before visit.
SEAP note: Mergui Archipelago pdf booklet plans on hold due to political situation.
Highlights: 800+ mostly uninhabited islands. Moken sea gypsies. Pristine reefs.
Like traveling back in time — complete solitude, exceptional natural beauty.
