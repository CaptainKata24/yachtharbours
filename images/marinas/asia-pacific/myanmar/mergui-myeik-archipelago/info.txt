MERGUI (MYEIK) ARCHIPELAGO
Location: Tanintharyi Coast, Southern Myanmar
Coordinates: 12.440N, 98.600E (central)
Area: 9,000+ square miles | Islands: 800+
Type: Remote cruising destination — no formal marinas
Entry Point: Kawthong (formerly Victoria Point) — southernmost Myanmar town
Check-in: Via Thai border from Ranong, Thailand | Advance notice strongly recommended
Stay: Permit required — arrange with licensed Myanmar yacht agent in advance
HIGHLIGHTS: Arguably Myanmar's most treasured and under-utilised tourism asset.
Mostly uninhabited islands. Moken (sea gypsies) semi-nomadic fishing communities.
Complete solitude, dozens of unspoiled anchorages. Jungles meeting silica beaches.
High-visibility snorkelling and diving. Abundant wildlife.
Best season: November-April (NE monsoon). Avoid May-October (SW monsoon).
CURRENT STATUS: Plans for Mergui Archipelago pdf booklet on hold due to Myanmar political situation.
Political unrest since 2021 coup — check FCO/State Dept advisories before visiting.
