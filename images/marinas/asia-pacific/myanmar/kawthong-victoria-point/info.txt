KAWTHONG (VICTORIA POINT)
Location: Southernmost tip of Myanmar, Tanintharyi Region
Coordinates: 10.015N, 98.553E
Type: Port of entry — border town opposite Ranong, Thailand
VHF: Monitor Ch16 | CIQ: Yes
Entry: Cross from Ranong, Thailand via Pak Nam ferry (10 min)
Immigration: Day-trip visa or full cruising permit available
HIGHLIGHTS: Southernmost point of Myanmar mainland.
Gateway to the Mergui Archipelago — 800+ islands north.
Ko Chang (Myanmar) immediately north — first anchorage.
Fuel and basic provisions available.
CAUTION: Check latest political situation and FCO/State Dept travel advisories.
Myanmar has been under military junta control since February 2021.
