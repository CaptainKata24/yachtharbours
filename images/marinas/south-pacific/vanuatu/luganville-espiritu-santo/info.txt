LUGANVILLE — ESPIRITU SANTO
Location: Luganville, Espiritu Santo Island, Vanuatu
Coordinates: 15.523S, 167.178E | VHF: Ch16
Type: Second largest town in Vanuatu — port and anchorage
CIQ: Yes (inter-island) | Fuel: Yes | Basic provisions: Yes
HIGHLIGHTS: SS President Coolidge — one of the world's best and most accessible
wreck dives. 22,000-ton luxury liner, sunk 1942. 70m deep, intact.
Million Dollar Point — WWII equipment dumped offshore, now spectacular dive site.
Champagne Beach — rated one of Pacific's best beaches.
Blue Holes — freshwater swimming in vivid blue natural pools.
Vanuatu's largest island. Active volcanoes on nearby Ambrym Island.
