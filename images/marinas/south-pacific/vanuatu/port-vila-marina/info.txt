PORT VILA MARINA
Location: Port Vila, Efate Island, Vanuatu
Coordinates: 17.738S, 168.321E | VHF: Ch16
Type: Main port of entry + yacht marina
CIQ: YES | Fuel: Yes | Water: Yes | Shore Power: Yes
Haul-out: Limited | Repairs: Available | F&B: Multiple in town
HIGHLIGHTS: Port Vila — one of the Pacific's most attractive capitals.
Mele Cascades waterfalls. Blue Lagoon swimming holes.
Duty-free port — excellent provisioning at affordable prices.
Remarkable French/British colonial heritage (former New Hebrides Condominium).
Ni-Vanuatu culture — kastom villages, sand drawing, tam-tam drums.
Regular flights to Australia, New Zealand, Fiji, New Caledonia.
Best season: April-October (SE trades — outside cyclone season).
Cyclone season: November-April — seek protected anchorage.
