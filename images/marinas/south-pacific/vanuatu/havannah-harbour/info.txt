HAVANNAH HARBOUR
Location: North Efate, Vanuatu
Coordinates: 17.583S, 168.233E
Type: Protected natural harbour — excellent anchorage
HIGHLIGHTS: One of Vanuatu's most sheltered anchorages. Beautiful mangroves.
Havannah Resort and Spa — luxury eco-resort accessible by yacht.
WWII history — major Allied base during Pacific campaign.
Excellent snorkelling on fringing reefs. Remote and peaceful.
Only 30 min by sea from Port Vila.
