VANUATU CRUISING OVERVIEW
Location: Southwest Pacific Ocean, 1,750km east of Australia
Islands: 13 main + many smaller, stretching 650km N-S
Capital: Port Vila (Efate Island)
Independence: 1980 (formerly Anglo-French New Hebrides Condominium)
Currency: Vatu (VUV) | Language: Bislama, English, French
Entry: Port Vila (main) or Luganville (Espiritu Santo)
Best season: April-October (SE trades, outside cyclone belt)
Cyclone season: November-April
Highlights: SS President Coolidge wreck dive. Active volcanoes (Tanna — Mt Yasur).
Champagne Beach. Blue Holes. Kastom villages. Duty-free shopping.
Yacht services: Moderate in Port Vila. Limited elsewhere.
