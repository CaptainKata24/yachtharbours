GIZO
Location: Western Province, Solomon Islands
Coordinates: 8.103S, 156.840E
Type: Town and anchorage — Western Province hub
HIGHLIGHTS: JFK — PT-109 story. John F. Kennedy swam from here after his boat
was sunk by Japanese destroyer in 1943. Kennedy Island nearby.
Excellent diving — Toa Maru WWII wreck. Fatboys Resort and dive centre.
Gizo market — fresh fish and vegetables. Friendly local community.
Gateway to the Marovo Lagoon and New Georgia group.
Regular flights from Honiara. Close to PNG border.
