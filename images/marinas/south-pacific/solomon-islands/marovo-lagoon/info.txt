MAROVO LAGOON
Location: Western and Central Province, Solomon Islands
Coordinates: 8.500S, 157.900E
Type: UNESCO World Heritage Site (nominated) — world's largest saltwater lagoon
HIGHLIGHTS: Double barrier reef lagoon — extraordinary marine biodiversity.
Traditional Malaitan woodcarving villages — renowned throughout Pacific.
Numerous small islands and anchorages within lagoon.
Excellent diving and snorkelling throughout. Dugongs present.
Traverse the lagoon from Seghe to Munda/Gizo — classic Solomon Islands passage.
Very few tourists — pristine, authentic Pacific island life.
