SOLOMON ISLANDS CRUISING OVERVIEW
Location: Southwest Pacific, east of Papua New Guinea
Nation: 6 main islands + 900+ smaller islands | Area: 28,400 sq km
Capital: Honiara (Guadalcanal) | Latitude: 5deg-13degS
Independence: 1978 (former British protectorate)
Currency: Solomon Islands Dollar | Language: English + 70+ local languages
Entry: Honiara (main) | Western Solomons (Gizo) secondary
Best season: May-October (SE trades)
Cyclone risk: November-April
Highlights: WWII Iron Bottom Sound wrecks. Marovo Lagoon (world's largest).
JFK/PT-109 history. Traditional culture. Outstanding diving throughout.
Marine biodiversity among the highest in the Pacific.
Yacht services: Basic in Honiara. Very limited elsewhere — be self-sufficient.
